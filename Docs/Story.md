# COMMANDERS: The Iron Winter — Story Bible

## The world

**1958, an alternate Cold War.** The last great war ended in an armistice, not a victory. A line was drawn down the middle of the continent along the **Varn River**.

* **The Western Concord** is a coalition of free nations headquartered in Geneva. It is democratic, technologically sophisticated, and stretched thin. Its armies favour mobility, precision artillery and air power.
* **The Iron Pact** is a vast eastern union forged from heavy industry and discipline. Its people remember the armistice as a theft. Its armour is slow but nearly unstoppable.

Twelve years of uneasy peace end on **17 December 1958**, when the Iron Pact crosses the Varn without a declaration of war.

### Aurora
Pact scientist **Dr. Ilse Varga** spent eleven years building **Aurora**, an ionospheric engine she was told would end droughts and famines. It is really a weapon. The **Aurora Lance** on **Mount Belov** can pin a continental storm over a target for weeks and freeze a nation into submission. Premier Vasko plans to fire it at Geneva at midnight on New Year's Eve. If Geneva does not kneel, he will fire it at the Pact's own city of **Volgrad** and blame the Concord.

The unnatural blizzards, rivers freezing overnight and strange rocket casings stamped with a sunburst seen throughout the campaign are Aurora's test firings.

## Characters

| Character | Side | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **The Commander** | Player | A newly promoted field commander on either side. Never seen and never speaks; defined by results. |
| **Gen. Adrian Hale** | Concord | Theatre commander. Gruff, honest, protective of civilians. Joins the final assault in person. |
| **Lt. Sarah Okafor** | Concord | Intelligence officer and the player's radio voice. Notices everything, including the frost on the rocket casings. |
| **Capt. Jonah Reyes** | Concord | 7th Cavalry and Ranger commander. Irreverent and brave. |
| **Dr. Ilse Varga** | Pact → defector | Aurora's architect. Ashamed of what she built. She defects to the Concord in *Operation Thaw*; in *The Long Night* her doubts are what first shake Volkova. |
| **Marshal Katya Volkova** | Pact | Field Marshal and the Pact player's superior. A proud, brilliant soldier who believes in the Pact's people, not its Premier. |
| **Commissar Lev Orlov** | Pact | Political officer. Begins as the Party's voice; ends the war standing with Volkova. |
| **Col. Gregor Strel** | Pact | Commander of the secretive Aurora Guard and Vasko's enforcer. The main antagonist of both campaigns. |
| **Premier Dmitri Vasko** | Pact | Ruler of the Iron Pact. Willing to sacrifice a city of his own to win a continent. Only ever heard, never seen. |

## Operation Thaw: the Concord campaign

| # | Mission | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ashfall** | At dusk the Pact vanguard hits the border town of Kessel. With a single platoon you keep the radio station broadcasting the evacuation order, hold until Reyes arrives, then silence a Thunder rocket battery. *Outro:* the casings are stamped **AURORA**. |
| 2 | **Foothold** | You deploy an MCV in the Valmont Valley, build your first base and destroy the Pact forward base. Radio intercepts reveal Strel refusing "Aurora support": *it is not ready.* |
| 3 | **Halvik Crossing** | You hold two bridges over the Lorne for twelve minutes against waves of armour, Vulture gunships and the first Titan siege tank. *Outro:* the river freezes solid in twenty minutes. |
| 4 | **Ghost Signal** | Reyes's strike team captures a listening post deep behind enemy lines. While the telemetry uploads, an encrypted message arrives from Dr. Varga: Aurora is a weapon, and she wants out. |
| 5 | **Iron Winter** | In a blizzard on the Karst Plateau you fight through to Station Nine, rescue Varga and escort her across the map to a waiting Hawk. She reveals the Lance's location and Vasko's deadline. |
| 6 | **Aurora** | Thirty minutes to midnight on Mount Belov. You break through the Pact army group, knock out the ring of power plants that feeds the Lance's Arc Towers, and destroy the Lance itself with Hale at your side. *Outro:* the storm breaks and the snow turns to rain. |

**Epilogue:** Varga's testimony topples Vasko's government. Hale retires, and his last dispatch reads: *"They held when holding was impossible."* Volkova is never found, which leaves room for a sequel.

## The Long Night: the Iron Pact campaign

| # | Mission | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **First Snow** | The opening blow: you destroy the Concord border post at Varn Ford so the main columns can cross unseen. Orlov warns that the word "Aurora" does not exist. |
| 2 | **The Iron Road** | You build a base and break the Concord garrison holding the Rhona Pass. An intercepted Hale admits the Pact commander "is good." |
| 3 | **Silence** | Against a fifteen-minute clock you destroy three Concord relay towers. Strel's Aurora Guard starts taking over commands, and Volkova pushes back. |
| 4 | **Sparks** | You defend the Aurora Assembly Site from Concord raids and Hawk strikes. Dr. Varga asks you whether you know what you are guarding; Strel silences her. Volkova admits she is afraid. |
| 5 | **Breaking the Line** | You storm Fort Bastion, the Concord's last fortress. Mid-battle, Volkova overhears Vasko on a secure line: if Geneva does not surrender, Volgrad, her mother's city, will be frozen and blamed on the Concord. |
| 6 | **Iron Verdict** | Volkova declares the order unlawful. Her loyalists, under your command, assault Mount Belov against Strel's Vasko Guard and seize the Aurora Control Center before midnight. Hale holds the Concord's fire and wishes her luck. *Outro:* Volkova orders every Pact unit to stand down. |

**Epilogue:** Vasko is arrested before dawn and Strel vanishes. The Lance is dismantled under joint observation. The war ends with a handshake on the Halvik bridge.

## Themes
* **Weather as a weapon, and the hubris of control.** Varga built a miracle and others aimed it.
* **Loyalty to people rather than leaders.** Volkova's arc and Orlov's turn.
* **Two honourable soldiers on opposite sides.** Hale and Volkova never meet, but each recognises the other.
* **Seeing the same war from both sides.** Missions mirror each other (Kessel/Varn Ford, Valmont/Rhona, Halvik/Fort Bastion), and both campaigns end at Mount Belov at midnight.
